An award-winning post-apocalyptic dystopian adventure where freedom comes at a cost, with over 1,500 copies sold worldwide. In a world changed by The Great Shift, the sinister Carnival Diablo is a symbol of terror and enslavement, ruled by the ruthless Ringmaster.

Two writers, one name

P V Bousfield is P (Peter) and V (Victoria), a couple in Stockton-on-Tees, writing together. They are the Bronze Award-winning authors of The Crimson Cage. Perfect for fans of The Hunger Games, Divergent and Shadow and Bone.

About P and V

P grew up in Stockton-on-Tees on fantasy, horror and post-apocalyptic worlds, and loves creating complex, morally grey characters that linger long after the final page.

V grew up in North Yorkshire surrounded by countryside and wild open spaces, with four sisters and a love of The Lord of the Rings. Together they live in Stockton-on-Tees with their dog, Hugo.
